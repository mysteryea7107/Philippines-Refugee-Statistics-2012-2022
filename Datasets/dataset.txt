1. OVERVIEW

This dataset contains records of refugee and asylum-seeker populations associated with the Philippines over an 11-year period (2012–2022). It comprises 722 total recorded individuals (512 refugees and 210 asylum-seekers) originating from 31 distinct countries.



2. VARIABLE DEFINITIONS & CLASSIFICATION

----------------------------------------------------------------------

Variable Name          Type          Subtype            Description

----------------------------------------------------------------------

Year                   Qualitative   Ordinal/Temporal   Year recorded (2012–2022)

Asylum Country         Qualitative   Nominal            Category/country in asylum field

Country-of-origin      Qualitative   Nominal            Country of origin

Country-of-asylum      Qualitative   Nominal            Country where asylum was sought/received

UNHCR-refugees         Quantitative  Discrete           Number of recorded refugees

Asylum-seekers         Quantitative  Discrete           Number of recorded asylum-seekers

Total-count            Quantitative  Discrete           Total recorded individuals in observation
